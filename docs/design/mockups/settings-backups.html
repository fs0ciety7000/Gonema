In plain words: every 7 days, Gonema saves the database and keeps the 4 newest scheduled copies, without the master key. Backups made by hand, uploaded or made before a restore stay until you delete them.

The master key

gonema.keyIt unlocks the secrets stored in the database: indexer keys, client passwords, the TMDB key.
  • Back it up once, somewhere else (a password manager, an offline copy). It never changes.
  • Left out of archives by default. An archive holding both the database and the key unlocks everything.
  • A backup restored with another key keeps its films and settings; its stored passwords must be entered again.
